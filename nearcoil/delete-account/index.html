Delete your NEARCOIL account

App: NEARCOIL: Snake Arcade (com.nikitapetrovapps.nearcoil)
Developer: Nikita Petrov Apps (Nikita Petrov)
Last updated: 2026-11-13

You can delete your NEARCOIL account and the data the game keeps on its server in two ways: in the game, or by e-mail without installing the game. Both delete the same data.

Option 1: in the game (immediate)

  1. Open NEARCOIL and go to Settings → ACCOUNT & SYNC (the same screen also opens from Profile).
  2. At the bottom, under DATA, tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap DELETE.

The phone needs an internet connection. The account and its data are deleted from the server at once; the game says ACCOUNT DELETED and starts over as a new anonymous player. If the connection fails, nothing is deleted and the game asks you to try again.

Option 2: by e-mail (no app needed)

Write to nikitapetrovapps@gmail.com with the subject NEARCOIL account deletion and tell us how to find your account:

  • If you saved your progress with Google or Apple (CONTINUE WITH GOOGLE / CONTINUE WITH APPLE): the e-mail address of that Google or Apple account. Please write from that address if you can; otherwise we may ask you to confirm from it.
  • Otherwise the account is anonymous: give the display name shown in the game, the country you chose (if any) and roughly when you last played.

We never ask for a password. We may ask one question to make sure the request comes from the account's owner. The account is deleted within 30 days of the request (usually much sooner), and you get a reply when it is done. If we cannot find the account, we tell you.

What is deleted

Deleting the account permanently removes from the game's server:

  • the account itself: the anonymous account id, sessions and sign-in tokens;
  • if you signed in with Google or Apple: the link to that account, its sign-in ID, the e-mail address and profile details received with the sign-in;
  • the cloud copy of your progress (Campaign, records, achievements, statistics counters, themes and purchased items);
  • your display name, country, leaderboard entries, league history, Ranked and Daily runs and their replays;
  • name reports you filed or that were filed against your name;
  • gameplay statistics and performance diagnostics;
  • messages you sent with SEND FEEDBACK, with their technical details;
  • the record of any tester or reviewer access code you used.

What is kept, and for how long

Purchase recordsThe game's record of each store purchase is kept for bookkeeping, but released from your account: the account id and country are removed. What remains is the item, list price and currency, the store's order number, the time, the platform, the game version and whether it was a test. Kept for up to 10 years, then deleted. Payment itself is handled by Google Play or the App Store, which keep their own records under their policies.
Anonymous global totalsTotals such as "food eaten by all players" contain no player and stay.
Security logs and backupsThe hosting provider's sign-in security logs (time, IP address, event) and encrypted database backups can still hold traces of the account until they expire, at most 30 days after the deletion. They are not used for anything else.
Your e-mails to usE-mails you sent (including EMAIL INSTEAD feedback) are deleted on request. A deletion request and our reply are kept for 12 months as a record that it was carried out.

Not affected by the deletion:

  • Data on your phone (Campaign progress, records, settings, replays, video clips) stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Your Google or Apple account itself. You can also remove NEARCOIL from the apps connected to it: Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services, or iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.
  • Data held by Google for ads (AdMob) and Google Play, or by Apple. The developer cannot access or delete it; see Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting some data without deleting the account

You can turn off gameplay statistics at any time in Settings → ACCOUNT & SYNC → SEND PLAY STATS. To have specific data deleted while keeping the account (for example your feedback messages or statistics), write to the same address and say what to delete.
